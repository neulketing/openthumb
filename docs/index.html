Your phone, running itself.

An on-device AI agent with a real Linux shell — that keeps working when you are not looking. Notification-driven runs, scheduled runs, and a whole fleet drivable from one host. Free and open source.

What makes it autonomous

Notification triggers

A message lands, the agent wakes and handles it — per-app rules with text matching, cooldowns, and loop protection. No tap required.

Scheduled runs

Prompts that fire on a clock — daily briefings, inbox sweeps, whatever you script. Survives reboots, runs headless with a completion note.

A real Linux shell

Sandboxed Alpine Linux on the phone itself. The agent installs packages, runs scripts, and works with real files.

Full device hands

Screen control via accessibility, notifications, clipboard, alarms — you grant the permissions, the agent does the work.

Fleet orchestration

Drive many phones from one host over adb + JSON-RPC with openthumb-fleet: one prompt, every device, concurrently.

Nothing collected

No analytics, no telemetry, no accounts, no server. Your keys and chats go only to the model provider you configure. Privacy

Standing on OpenMinis

OpenThumb is a GPL-3.0 fork of OpenMinis, and nearly all of the app is their work. If you want one great conversational agent on your own phone — better documented, actively released, on the App Store — use OpenMinis. OpenThumb exists for the part upstream does not target: phones that act on their own, alone or as a fleet.
